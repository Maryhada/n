Football

Football is a field event played by eleven players but in total 22 players.I am not saying this to persude u to join a football club.The word "football" doesn't refer to a single sport but to a family of team sports that involve, to varying degrees, kicking a ball to score a goal. The most common element is scoring goals or points by moving the ball to an opposing team's end of the field. Interestingly, the name itself is debated; it might refer to kicking the ball with the foot, or to the fact these games were played on foot, rather than on horseback.Globally, there are many different types of football, which can be broadly split into kicking codes (like soccer, where handling the ball is limited) and carrying codes (like American football and rugby, where carrying the ball by hand is common).
